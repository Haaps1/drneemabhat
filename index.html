About the Doctor

A decade of dedicated hematology-oncology care

Dr. Neema Bhat is a Haemato-Oncologist based in Bangalore, with more than ten years of specialised experience treating blood disorders and blood cancers in both children and adults. She completed her MD in the United States, followed by a fellowship (FAAP) in Pediatric Hematology, Oncology & Bone Marrow Transplantation at Penn State Health — training that shapes how she diagnoses, stages and treats every patient who comes to her.

She currently serves as Program Director and Head of the Bone Marrow Transplant Unit at Bhagawan Mahaveer Jain Hospital, in partnership with Sankalp India Foundation, and has previously practised at BGS Gleneagles Global Hospitals and Fortis Hospitals in Bangalore.

Fluent in English, Hindi, Kannada, Telugu and Tamil, she is known for a patient-centric approach that prioritises clear communication and treatment plans built around each individual’s needs, rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol.
