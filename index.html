About Dr. Neema Bhat

A decade of dedicated hematology-oncology care

Dr. Neema Bhat is a Haemato-Oncologist based in Bangalore, with more than ten years of specialised experience treating blood disorders and blood cancers in both children and adults. She completed her MD in the United States, followed by a fellowship (FAAP) in Pediatric Hematology, Oncology & Bone Marrow Transplantation at Penn State Health — training that shapes how she diagnoses, stages and treats every patient who comes to her.

She currently serves as Program Director and Head of the Bone Marrow Transplant Unit at Bhagawan Mahaveer Jain Hospital, in partnership with Sankalp India Foundation, and has previously practised at BGS Gleneagles Global Hospitals and Fortis Hospitals in Bangalore.

Her work spans benign and malignant blood conditions — from anaemia, thalassemia and bleeding disorders to leukaemia, lymphoma and childhood solid tumours — along with autologous and allogeneic stem cell transplantation for conditions such as aplastic anaemia and immunodeficiency disorders.

Fluent in English, Hindi, Kannada, Telugu and Tamil, she is known for a patient-centric approach that prioritises clear communication and treatment plans built around each individual’s needs, rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol.

Bone Marrow Transplantation

A second chance, carefully planned

A bone marrow (stem cell) transplant replaces diseased or damaged marrow with healthy blood-forming cells. For many blood cancers, thalassemia, aplastic anaemia and immune disorders, it offers the best chance of long-term cure.

Uses the patient’s own stem cells, collected and stored before high-dose therapy. Often used for lymphoma, myeloma and some solid tumours.

Uses stem cells from a matched donor — a sibling, relative or unrelated volunteer. Used for leukaemia, thalassemia, aplastic anaemia and immunodeficiency.

Dr. Bhat leads the Bone Marrow Transplant Unit at Bhagawan Mahaveer Jain Hospital in partnership with Sankalp India Foundation, with experience across both benign and malignant conditions in children and adults.
